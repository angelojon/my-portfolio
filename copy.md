## Rules for anything written later

- Banned: passionate, high-quality, exceptional user experiences, bringing
  visions to life, leveraging, cutting-edge, seamless, innovative, digital
  experiences, craft, meticulous attention to detail
- Sentence case. No ALL CAPS taglines.
- If a sentence could be deleted without losing information, delete it.
- Name real things. "A bike configurator with compatibility logic" beats
  "e-commerce solutions."
